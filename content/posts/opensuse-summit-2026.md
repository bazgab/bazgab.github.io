## openSUSE America Summit 2026

This past month I was fortunate enough to speak at the openSUSE America Summit 2026 in Barranquilla, Colombia, together with many SUSE colleagues, as well as the wonderful LinuxBQ Community, JFrog, Astian, and others. 

I talked about git-based packaging (if interested, see the previous article) and had great insights from speakers in topics such as testing with Kuttl, LLM security issues and locally-developed agricultural solutions. It was an honor to have a great talk with fellow openSUSE colleagues Douglas DeMaio, Johannes Segitz and Patrick Fitzgerald, as well as the lovely reception by Alfonso Hernandez and Carlos Reyes, I look forward to meeting with you guys again in the future!
